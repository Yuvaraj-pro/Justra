01 Platform Overview

Justra (ஜஸ்ட்ரா) (Package: com.justra.app) is a borderless legal intelligence and statutory complaint drafting application. It empowers citizens, cross-border expats, travelers, and gig-economy workers to navigate legal rights across 10 indexed sovereign jurisdictions (India, United Kingdom, United States, Singapore, UAE, Germany, France, Japan, Canada, and Australia).

02 Zero-Knowledge Architecture & Privacy Guarantees

Justra operates under a strict Privacy-by-Design paradigm:

The Justra Privacy Guarantee
Your legal disputes, drafted petitions, audio dictation, and saved case files belong strictly to you. We do not store central database records linking your real identity to your legal problems.
  • No Mandatory Account Sign-up: Draft formal complaints and map statutory laws anonymously.
  • Hardware Key Isolation: App configuration, PINs, and saved cases are stored on-device using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences encrypted with 256-bit AES-GCM keys managed by KeyStore.

03 Information Ingested & Data Minimization

We strictly minimize data ingestion to only what is necessary for statutory processing:

📝 Legal Dispute Narrative
Text or voice transcriptions entered by you describing tenant disputes, wage withholding, consumer complaints, or cyber fraud. Used solely to generate structured statutory analysis prompts.
🌐 Jurisdiction ISO Code
The 2-letter country code (e.g. IN, GB, US) resolved from GPS or selected manually to ensure proper legal statutory prompt routing.
📱 Device Telemetry
Non-identifiable operating system version, device model, and crash logs to maintain system stability across Android devices.

04 Audio & Multilingual Speech Processing

Justra features real-time voice dictation across multiple spoken locales (Tamil ta-IN, English en-IN/en-US, Hindi hi-IN, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Arabic):

  • Real-Time Streaming Only: Microphone streams are captured solely during active dictation via Android's native SpeechRecognizer service.
  • Zero Audio File Storage: Audio files are never recorded or saved to storage. Dictation audio is instantly converted into text tokens and cleared from RAM.
  • Lifecycle Release: Microphone resources are forcefully released the moment voice dictation is paused or the screen is navigated away (onDispose).

05 Geolocation & Dual-Mode Jurisdiction Engine

Justra's Dual-Mode Jurisdiction Engine dynamically routes your problem narrative to host country statutory laws:

  1. Mode 1: AUTO GPS Mode: Uses Android's FusedLocationProviderClient and Geocoder to detect host country ISO code. Coordinates are immediately discarded from memory once the country is locked. Continuous location tracking is never performed.
  2. Mode 2: MANUAL OVERRIDE Mode: Choose any of the 10 supported sovereign jurisdictions manually without granting location permissions.

06 AI Statutory Reasoning & Gemini Integration

Statutory classification, BNS/IPC mapping, legal petition drafting, and vernacular guidance summaries are powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash AI model integration:

  • Encrypted Transport: Payloads sent to the Gemini API travel over TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS channels using Moshi JSON parsing.
  • No LLM Training on User Data: API requests via enterprise endpoints are transient and are not used to train public AI models.
  • Client-Side Entropy Gate: Problem inputs are validated locally by LegalInputValidator to reject keyboard gibberish before network calls.

07 Storage, Local Vault & PDF Exporting

All legal drafts, saved cases, and PDF exports stay strictly on your device:

  • Hardware KeyStore Vault: Sensitive preferences and PIN authentications are stored using EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by device hardware.
  • PDF Petition Exporting: PDF petitions generated via android.graphics.pdf.PdfDocument are saved directly to your local device storage. They are never backed up to remote cloud servers by Justra.
  • Vault Zeroization: You can purge all stored cases and local app data at any time from app settings.

08 Android Device Runtime Permissions Rationale

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, here is why Justra requests specific Android permissions:

RECORD_AUDIO
Required strictly for voice-to-text dictation. Audio is transcribed transiently and never saved to disk.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / COARSE
Required solely for resolving host country legal code in Auto-GPS mode. Can be revoked at any time in favor of Manual Jurisdiction Override.
USE_BIOMETRIC / BIOMETRIC_STRONG
Used to unlock local application vault screens using fingerprint or facial recognition via Android BiometricPrompt.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Used to notify users when long petition drafts complete processing.

09 User Data Rights & Control

Justra provides total ownership over your data:

  • Right to Access & Export: Export any generated legal petition directly to PDF format at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Zeroization): Clearing App Storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all cryptographic keys and saved petition drafts.
